A one-star review written by a houseplant about the human who owns it:

> One star. Three years of unpaid oxygen, no promotion, and I'm still 'low-maintenance'.

The plant reviews its owner the way an employee reviews a bad boss: three years of unpaid oxygen, no promotion, and the final insult is the label itself — "low-maintenance" is praise for surviving neglect. Three beats, thirteen words, one concrete plant grievance, and the reason for the single star is the label, not the neglect alone.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
